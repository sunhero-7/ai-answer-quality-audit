Six indivisible display items have these masses: A 6 kg, B 5 kg, C 4 kg, D 3 kg, E 2 kg and F 2 kg. Use exactly two boxes. Each box may contain at most three items and at most 12 kg of items; ignore the box's own mass. Put every item in exactly one box. Items A and B must be in different boxes, and items E and F must stay together.

Give one valid packing, listing the items and total item mass in each box. Briefly show that all constraints are satisfied. Do not split items or add boxes.
