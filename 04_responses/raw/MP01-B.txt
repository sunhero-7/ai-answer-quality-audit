Time spent driving:

\[
t_1=\frac{600}{4.0}=150\text{ s},\qquad
t_2=\frac{900}{6.0}=150\text{ s}
\]

Including the stop, total time is \(150+90+150=390\text{ s}\).

Total distance is \(600+900=1500\text{ m}\).

\[
\text{Average speed}=\frac{1500}{390}=3.84615\ldots
=\boxed{3.85\text{ m/s}}
\]

Converting the unrounded value to km/h:

\[
\frac{1500}{390}\times3.6=13.84615\ldots
=\boxed{13.85\text{ km/h}}
