Evening classes are for participants aged 16 or over. The class must take place on or before your voucher’s printed expiry date; booking before expiry isn’t enough.

Please send your nephew’s age, your intended class date and the voucher’s expiry date so support can check a suitable booking.

If your nephew is aged 8–15, he can attend a family class with a paying adult instead.

Vouchers can be redeemed against either class, subject to age rules and availability, but don’t reserve a seat. I can’t check availability, reserve a place or confirm one here.
