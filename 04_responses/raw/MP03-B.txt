April: \(250 \times 0.80 = \boxed{200\text{ kWh}}\).

May: \(200 \times 1.20 = \boxed{240\text{ kWh}}\).

May’s net percentage change: \(\frac{240-250}{250}\times100=\boxed{-4\%}\), a 4% decrease.

The changes do not cancel because the 20% increase is applied to April’s smaller amount, so it adds only 40 kWh after the decrease removed 50 kWh.
