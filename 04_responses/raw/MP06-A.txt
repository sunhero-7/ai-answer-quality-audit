Using **energy = power × time**:

- **Lamp A:** \(12 \times 5 = 60\text{ Wh}\)  
  \(60 \div 1{,}000 = 0.060\text{ kWh}\)

- **Lamp B:** \(8 \times 8 = 64\text{ Wh}\)  
  \(64 \div 1{,}000 = 0.064\text{ kWh}\)

**Lamp B uses more energy**, by:
\[
64 - 60 = 4\text{ Wh}
\]
\[
4 \times 3{,}600 = 14{,}400\text{ J}
\]

Lower power does not necessarily mean lower total energy use because energy also depends on how long the lamp operates. Lamp B’s longer operating time outweighs its lower power.
