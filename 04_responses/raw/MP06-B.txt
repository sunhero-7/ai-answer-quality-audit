Using **energy = power × time**:

- **Lamp A:** \(12 \text{ W} \times 5 \text{ h} = 60 \text{ Wh} = 0.060 \text{ kWh}\).
- **Lamp B:** \(8 \text{ W} \times 8 \text{ h} = 64 \text{ Wh} = 0.064 \text{ kWh}\).

**Lamp B uses more energy** by:
\[
64 - 60 = 4 \text{ Wh}
\]
\[
4 \times 3{,}600 = 14{,}400 \text{ J}
\]

Lower power means a lower rate of energy use, but total energy also depends on operating time. Lamp B runs long enough to use more energy overall.
