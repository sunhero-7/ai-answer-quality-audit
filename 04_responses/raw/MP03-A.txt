April: \(250 \times 0.80 = 200\text{ kWh}\).

May: \(200 \times 1.20 = 240\text{ kWh}\).

Net change: \(\frac{240-250}{250}\times100=-4\%\), so May’s use was **4% below** the original.

The changes do not cancel because the 20% increase applies to April’s lower amount, adding only 40 kWh after a decrease of 50 kWh.
