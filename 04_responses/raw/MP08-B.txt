After drawing one blue counter, **3 blue counters remain out of 5 counters**, so the probability of a second blue is \(3/5\), not \(4/6\). The first counter is not replaced.

- **Both blue:**
  \[
  \frac46\times\frac35=\frac25=\boxed{40\%}.
  \]

- **At least one orange:** This is the complement of both being blue:
  \[
  1-\frac25=\frac35=\boxed{60\%}.
  \]
