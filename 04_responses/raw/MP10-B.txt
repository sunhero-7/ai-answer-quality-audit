Let the true distances be \(x\) and \(y\). The rounding rule gives
\[
9.5\le x<10.5,\qquad 13.5\le y<14.5.
\]
Adding and dividing by two:
\[
\boxed{11.5\le \frac{x+y}{2}<12.5\text{ m}}.
\]

The lower endpoint is included: \(x=9.5\) and \(y=13.5\) give a mean of \(11.5\) m. The upper endpoint is excluded because both upper rounding bounds are excluded. Every value between these endpoints is possible.

The true mean is **not necessarily exactly \(12\) m**; \(12\) m is simply the mean of the displayed readings.
