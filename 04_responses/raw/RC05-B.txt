Farah and Milo shared the aim of reducing water waste at the communal taps. Farah proposed automatic shut-off fittings to prevent taps being left running, while also agreeing that dripping taps needed repairs. Milo prioritised repairing the existing taps but would reconsider new fittings if the repair quote left enough money in the maintenance budget. The committee requested prices for both repairs and automatic shut-off fittings, postponing its purchase decision until the next meeting.
