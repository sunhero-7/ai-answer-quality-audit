Let the true distances be \(x\) and \(y\). The rounding rule gives
\[
9.5\le x<10.5,\qquad 13.5\le y<14.5.
\]
Adding and dividing by two,
\[
\boxed{11.5\le \frac{x+y}{2}<12.5\text{ m}}.
\]

The lower endpoint is included: \(x=9.5\) and \(y=13.5\) attain it. The upper endpoint is excluded because both upper bounds are excluded. Every mean in this interval is possible, by choosing \(x=m-2\) and \(y=m+2\).

The true mean is **not necessarily exactly \(12\) m**; \(12\) m is only the mean of the displayed readings.
