No. Averaging the two group means is justified only when the groups have equal numbers of participants.

Group A’s total completion time is \(12\times8=96\) minutes. If Group B has \(n\ge1\) participants, the combined mean is

\[
\frac{96+11n}{12+n}\text{ minutes}.
\]

This equals \(9.5\) minutes exactly when \(n=12\). The missing information is Group B’s number of participants.
