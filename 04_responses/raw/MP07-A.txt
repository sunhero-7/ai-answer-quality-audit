The block’s volume is

\[
V=6.0\times4.0\times2.5=60\ \text{cm}^3.
\]

Since \(1\ \text{cm}=0.01\ \text{m}\),

\[
1\ \text{cm}^3=(0.01\ \text{m})^3=10^{-6}\ \text{m}^3,
\]
so
\[
V=60\times10^{-6}=0.000060\ \text{m}^3.
\]

The conversion factor is cubed because volume multiplies **three lengths**: each dimension contributes a factor of \(0.01\).

The density is

\[
\rho=\frac{180\ \text{g}}{60\ \text{cm}^3}
=3\ \text{g/cm}^3.
\]

Converting the mass, \(180\ \text{g}\div1000=0.180\ \text{kg}\), gives

\[
\rho=\frac{0.180\ \text{kg}}{0.000060\ \text{m}^3}
=3000\ \text{kg/m}^3.
\]

Thus, **volume = \(60\ \text{cm}^3=0.000060\ \text{m}^3\)** and **density = \(3\ \text{g/cm}^3=3000\ \text{kg/m}^3\)**.
