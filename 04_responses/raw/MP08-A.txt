The probability that **both counters are blue** is

\[
\frac{4}{6}\times\frac{3}{5}=\frac{2}{5}=40\%.
\]

After the first blue counter is drawn, only **3 blue counters remain out of 5 counters**, so the second blue probability is \(3/5\), not \(4/6\).

“At least one is orange” is the opposite of “both are blue”, so its probability is

\[
1-\frac{2}{5}=\frac{3}{5}=60\%.
\]
