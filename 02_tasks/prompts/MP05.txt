Use average acceleration = (final velocity − initial velocity) / elapsed time and average net force = mass × average acceleration. Answer in exactly three numbered points:
1. Calculate the average acceleration with units and direction.
2. Calculate the average net force with units and direction.
3. Explain why subtracting the speeds 2.0 and 1.0 would be wrong here. Show the essential working within the relevant points.
