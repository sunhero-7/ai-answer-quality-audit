Prepare exactly 18 identical workshop kits. Each kit needs two pencils, three blank cards and one envelope. Existing stock contains 9 pencils, 20 blank cards and 5 envelopes. New supplies are sold only in whole packs: pencils in packs of 10, cards in packs of 25 and envelopes in packs of 8. Assume all stock is usable.

Buy the smallest possible number of packs of each supply that allows all 18 kits to be assembled. Give a table with one row per supply and these columns: total needed, existing stock, packs to buy, items left after assembly. Show or explain how you rounded the shortfall to whole packs. Do not substitute items between kits.
